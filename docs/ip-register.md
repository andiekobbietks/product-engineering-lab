## Specific patent-source note

Google Patents lists GB2591502A as “Hair applicator”, names Mouhamad Youmna as inventor and Myana Naturals Ltd as assignee, and displays an “Expired - Fee Related” status. The same page warns that its legal-status and assignee data may be inaccurate and are not legal conclusions. This register therefore treats those fields as discovery metadata only; confirm them against the official UK IPO register before relying on ownership, enforceability, expiry or commercial rights. The family-level association with Nyfasi is strongly corroborated: the Royal Academy of Engineering identifies Dr Youmna Mouhamad as Nyfasi's founder and describes the Deluxe Detangler as Nyfasi's patented technology, while *Le Journal de Mayotte* describes it as Nyfasi's patented innovation. The exact mapping from GB2591502A to a specific retail SKU/revision remains unverified because those sources do not cite the publication number. Do not treat the patent metadata alone as proof of the exact commercial product configuration. The patent drawing locators are recorded separately in the figure register.

## Patent and design caution

Patent drawings illustrate embodiments and claim context; they do not establish that a pictured embodiment is the exact retail product, nor do they provide manufacturing tolerances or a complete bill of materials. A reconstruction from public evidence is not a freedom-to-operate opinion. Do not manufacture, sell, import or market a reconstruction without qualified engineering review and legal clearance in relevant territories.

## Evidence record minimum

For every source or artefact, capture stable ID/title; canonical URL and access date; publisher/creator/claimed owner; asset type and jurisdiction; licence or permission evidence; exact files/excerpts and hashes where practical; transformations, prompts and tool/model versions; attribution and redistribution constraints; confidence, unresolved questions and reviewer; and allowed uses (inspect, quote, display, adapt, train, distribute, commercialise).

Do not change a record from unverified to cleared based only on search snippets, a public URL or an assumption that patent drawings are free to reuse. Attach the relevant licence, permission, official register extract or written legal assessment.
