# Commercial proposal structure

**Positioning:** independent product-engineering research and a proposed route to a separately scoped R&D engagement. **Status:** framework only. No offer, fee, deliverable commitment, partnership or endorsement exists until agreed in writing.

## 1. Executive summary

- Client/sponsor: [Nyfasi / investor / product-category organisation]
- Prepared by: AndieKobbieTech
- Working title: Evidence-led product reconstruction and engineering intelligence
- Decision requested: discovery meeting and permission to assess a defined evidence pack
- First phase: paid discovery and feasibility sprint

## 2. Problem to validate

Product knowledge may be distributed across marketing imagery, patent publications, design case studies, supplier documents, physical samples and fragmented CAD. Validate through interviews whether converting those sources into an evidence-linked, editable product model reduces a specific cost or risk. Do not claim a customer has this problem until interviews and baseline evidence support it.

## 3. Proposed outcome

A traceable engineering dossier containing, where authorised: source and rights register; component taxonomy and product data model; annotated reference views; parameterised, explicitly approximate 3D reconstruction; exploded assembly and component relationships; assumptions, unknowns and confidence ratings; comparison protocol against authorised photographs or measurements; validation findings and next-stage recommendation.

## 4. Work packages

| Work package | Activities | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| WP0 — Rights and data intake | Confirm authority, permitted sources, confidentiality, formats and retention | Signed scope/data schedule; approved source list |
| WP1 — Evidence modelling | Source register, provenance schema, component taxonomy, uncertainty register | Reviewable evidence ledger with traceable claims |
| WP2 — Reconstruction prototype | Sand3D parametric components, assembly and export | Reproducible script, pinned environment, exported geometry |
| WP3 — Validation | Compare against authorised images, measurements or sample | Measurement protocol, error table, limitations and reviewer sign-off |
| WP4 — Productisation assessment | Workflow architecture, integration, security and operations | Architecture decision record, risk register and roadmap |
| WP5 — Handover | Documentation, reproducibility, training and agreed source/asset transfer | Handover pack and agreed licence/ownership schedule |

## 5. Scope boundaries

Unless added in a signed statement of work, exclude manufacturing-ready CAD, tolerances, tooling or certification; freedom-to-operate, patent validity or infringement opinions; unauthorised reverse engineering; copying third-party assets without permission; production safety/regulatory certification; guaranteed geometry accuracy from photographs alone; and transfer of pre-existing AK Build or AndieKobbieTech background IP.

## 6. Commercial model

1. **Paid discovery:** fixed fee, limited duration, agreed evidence pack and decision report.
2. **Prototype R&D:** milestone-based fee with acceptance criteria and change control.
3. **Implementation:** time-and-materials or fixed-price work packages after uncertainty is reduced.
4. **Ongoing platform/service:** subscription or support agreement for managed workflows, hosting, integrations and service levels, if justified.
5. **Optional licence:** separately negotiated licence to specifically identified deliverables or foreground IP. Exclusivity, field-of-use, territory, sublicensing or royalty terms require explicit valuation and legal review.

Do not quote prices until effort, data readiness, liability, insurance, delivery risk and customer requirements have been assessed.

## 7. Intellectual property schedule

The contract should distinguish background IP (pre-existing tools, methods, libraries, templates and know-how); foreground IP (commissioned outputs); third-party IP (software, datasets, images, patents, CAD); customer data; expressly identified open components; and commercial components.

**Suggested negotiation position, not a statement of legal default:** each party retains its background IP; customer receives expressly agreed rights to paid deliverables; provider retains reusable tools and general know-how; third-party rights remain with their owners. The final contract must define ownership, licences, access after termination, warranties, liability, confidentiality, data handling and permitted publicity.

## 8. Security and data handling

Specify approved transfer channel, access control, encryption, data residency, retention/deletion, backups, subprocessors, incident notification, personal data status and whether customer material may be used for model training. Default position: no customer confidential material or personal data is used for training or public demonstrations without express written authorisation.

## 9. Governance and acceptance

Name business sponsor and technical reviewer; hold weekly review and decision log; tie acceptance to evidence rather than subjective visual similarity alone; use change requests for new sources, components, fidelity or outputs; set stop/go gates after discovery and prototype validation.

## 10. Success measures

Agree baseline and target with the customer. Candidate measures: time to assemble an evidence pack; proportion of model features with provenance; unresolved high-impact assumptions; geometry deviation against measured references; time to revise a component; and reproducibility of exports.

## 11. Proposed next step

A 60-minute discovery session to establish product scope, source authority, intended use, target fidelity, available CAD/sample material and decision-maker. Follow with a short paid discovery statement of work if there is a viable, authorised problem.

## 12. Appendices

A. Evidence and source register; B. IP and licence schedule; C. Deliverables and acceptance matrix; D. Assumptions and exclusions; E. Project plan and dependencies; F. Fees, payment milestones and expenses; G. Security/data-processing schedule; H. Change control and termination.
