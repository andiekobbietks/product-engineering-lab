# Architecture decision records

These records capture durable engineering decisions for Product Engineering Lab. They distinguish current architecture from historical experiments and state the trade-offs that would justify revisiting a decision.

| ADR | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [ADR-0001](adr-0001-sand3d-canonical-engine.md) | Adopt Sand3D as the canonical Nyfasi modelling and assembly engine | Accepted |
| [ADR-0002](adr-0002-sand3d-source-linked-gallery.md) | Replace the custom mesh viewer with a Sand3D-native, source-linked gallery | Accepted |

A decision is not evidence that a design is physically validated. The Nyfasi model remains a research proxy with explicit provenance and uncertainty.
