# Experiment 001 — Nyfasi Deluxe Detangler

This is an **independent research case study**. It is not commissioned by, affiliated with or endorsed by Nyfasi, Dr Youmna Mouhamad, PDR or iF Design.

The experiment explores how public product evidence can be converted into an auditable product model without confusing published claims, visual observations and engineering assumptions.

See the [image inventory](image-inventory.md), the [experiment record](https://github.com/andiekobbietks/product-engineering-lab/blob/main/experiments/001-nyfasi-reconstruction/README.md), [source register](https://github.com/andiekobbietks/product-engineering-lab/blob/main/experiments/001-nyfasi-reconstruction/evidence/source-register.json) and [uncertainty register](https://github.com/andiekobbietks/product-engineering-lab/blob/main/experiments/001-nyfasi-reconstruction/models/uncertainty-register.json).

## Patent evidence now mapped

The published GB2591502A disclosure describes a body enclosing a reservoir, a rotatable roller, a row of teeth, a curved wall with arc-shaped slots, and upper/lower housing portions. Its drawing description lists an exploded perspective, exterior views, roller detail and two cross-sections. The patent metadata names Mouhamad Youmna and Myana Naturals Ltd, but the direct relationship to the Nyfasi-branded Deluxe Detangler retail SKU remains unverified. These are patent-embodiment disclosures, not proof of the exact retail configuration. See the [patent reading notes](patent-reading-notes.md) and [figure-level register](https://github.com/andiekobbietks/product-engineering-lab/blob/main/experiments/001-nyfasi-reconstruction/evidence/patent-figure-register.csv).

The machine-readable model records `identity_mapping_status: unverified`; the current Sand3D proxy is named after GB2591502A rather than asserting a confirmed Nyfasi product mapping.

## Research outputs

- Evidence-linked product data model.
- Component-level illustrative Sand3D reconstruction.
- Exploded assembly and exportable geometry.
- Image/measurement comparison protocol.
- Explicit assumptions and unresolved dimensions.

## Non-claims

No original Nyfasi CAD has been established as available. The prototype is not manufacturing CAD, a verified product replica, a freedom-to-operate assessment or evidence of a commercial relationship. Third-party assets remain with their respective rights holders.
