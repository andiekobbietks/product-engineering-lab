# Licensing status

**Project-wide public licence: undecided.** The repository previously contained an MIT licence file, which was removed to avoid granting reuse rights before the IP review. Until a licence is approved, original repository material is not offered under an open-source licence merely because it is publicly visible.

The hybrid commercial direction is a design intention, not a current licence grant:

- Potential public layer: evidence schemas, provenance conventions, evaluation fixtures and non-sensitive adapters. Apache-2.0 is a candidate, not selected.
- Potential commercial layer: AK Build orchestration, private integrations, validation workflows and managed services, governed by separate contracts.
- Third-party layer: Nyfasi, patent, PDR, iF Design, Sand3D and dataset materials remain governed by their respective owners' rights and licences.

See the repository-root [LICENSING.md](https://github.com/andiekobbietks/product-engineering-lab/blob/main/LICENSING.md) for the full position and approval checklist. This is not legal advice.
