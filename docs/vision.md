# Vision

## Research question

Can fragmented product evidence be transformed into structured, traceable and testable engineering knowledge without losing the distinction between what is known and what is inferred?

Product knowledge is distributed across photographs, patents, manuals, CAD, supplier documents, requirements and user observations. The lab investigates repeatable ways to reconcile these sources while preserving provenance and uncertainty.

## Intended outcomes

- Evidence-linked product and component models.
- Machine-readable requirements, assumptions and unknowns.
- Editable geometry and assembly representations with confidence boundaries.
- Reproducible comparison and validation.
- Portable engineering knowledge across tools and model providers.
- A foundation for governed product engineering automation.

This is not a promise that AI can infer exact CAD from one photograph, a replacement for engineering sign-off, or a service for copying protected products.
