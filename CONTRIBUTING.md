# Contributing

Evidence before inference. Preserve source URLs, retrieval dates and exact locators. Distinguish `observed`, `documented`, `inferred`, `assumed`, `unknown` and `disputed`. Never silently promote assumptions into specifications.

Every experiment should include its research question, scope, source register, evidence ledger, machine-readable model where relevant, uncertainty register, reproduction steps, dependency versions, validation results, limitations and rights notes.

Prefer portable formats and provider-neutral adapters. Record tool/model versions. Require human review for consequential engineering decisions.

## Rights and licensing while undecided

The repository currently has no project-wide licence. Do not assume that public visibility grants permission to reuse original material. Contributions are not accepted under an implied open-source licence. Before submitting third-party images, drawings, datasets, CAD or code, document the rights basis and obtain permission where required. Do not submit material copied from a client, employer, university or another project unless you have authority to do so. The maintainer must approve a contribution and its intended licence before it is merged.
