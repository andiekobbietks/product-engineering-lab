## Use and rights boundary

The selected programmes were inspected as modelling-pattern references. They are not Nyfasi geometry or dimensional evidence. The extracted programme source remains in the temporary workflow artifact; this repository stores IDs, hashes, attribution and the intended pattern mapping, not the raw third-party source files or source meshes.

No gear train, pump mechanism, bearing type or oil-seal geometry has been asserted as part of the Nyfasi product. Those remain optional design hypotheses unless confirmed by patent evidence, authorised CAD or a physical sample.
